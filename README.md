# Primer

A World of Warcraft addon for Classic Era and World of Warcraft: Forever: a
class guide to read out of combat.

- **Play.** The opening and the order to press your spells in, for your level
  and your build, each line with its reason.
- **Talents.** The next points to take, in order, and why.
- **Role.** What your build is for.

Not a tracker: it reads nothing in combat, and it says plainly that it is a
sensible way to play and not the only one. English only for now. `/primer`
opens it.

The game's data comes from [wow-data](https://github.com/salty-max/wow-data):
the game clients' tables. The guide is original.
